Privacy Policy — Magic Crystal Shatter

Last updated 10 September 2026 · Onsaga Softworks · com.onsagasoftworks.magiccrystalshatter

The short version. Magic Crystal Shatter has no accounts, no servers of its own and no analytics. Your progress is saved on your own device, and apart from your phone's own backup, if you have one switched on, only two things ever leave it: the data Google AdMob collects to show adverts, and what Apple or Google handles when you buy something. We never see your name, your email or your payment details.

What stays on your device

Everything the game remembers about you is stored locally, on the phone or tablet you play on, in the system's app preferences. It is not uploaded to us, backed up to us, or shared:

Stored asWhat it holds
MND_SAVE_V1 Your progress and inventory — the highest level reached, total score, coins, gems, the power-ups you hold (bombs, shuffles, extra moves), whether you have already bought the one-time Starter Bundle, and your language choice
mnd_level_state_v1 The board of a level you left unfinished, so you can pick it up where you stopped
mnd_stars_<level> The stars you earned on each level
mnd_music_on, mnd_music_vol, mnd_sfx_on, mnd_sfx_vol, mnd_vibration Your music, sound and vibration settings
mnd_mech_seen_<type>, mnd_mech_lvl_<type>, mnd_mech_reminder_lvl Which special stones have already been introduced to you, so the explanation is not shown again
mnd_free_gems_level The level at which you were last offered the free-gems video, so the offer only comes round once every few dozen levels
unity.*, unity_connect.* A random installation ID, session IDs and a session counter that the Unity game engine keeps for its own online services. The game does not switch any of those services on, so none of this is sent to Unity or to us

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We keep no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on request. One thing worth knowing: if your phone's own backup is switched on — Google backup on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone — the system may include this data in your device backup and bring it back when you set up a phone from that backup. That backup belongs to your Google or Apple account; we never see it.

Advertising

The only adverts in the game are videos you choose to watch, in return for something: double the coins at the end of a level, extra moves when you run out, or — now and then, when you are short — free gems. There are no adverts between levels and none you did not ask for. The videos are served by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob collects device-level data — typically an advertising identifier, IP address, coarse device and OS information, and how you interacted with the advert. That collection is Google's, under Google's privacy policy, not ours: we receive no personal data from it.

  • Resetting the advertising identifier: Android — Settings → Google → Ads. iOS — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's own consent form before it requests any advert. If you agree, the adverts may be personalised to you. If you refuse, you can still watch the videos, but Google shows only adverts that are not personalised. Your answer is saved on your device, so the form does not come back every time you play.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. Your payment details go to them and never pass through the game. The game receives only a confirmation that a purchase completed, and adds what you bought to the save on your device. We do not know who bought what.

Permissions

On Android the game itself declares two permissions. VIBRATE is for the short vibration when a level ends, which you can switch off in Settings. INTERNET is used by the advertising SDK and by the store's purchase service; the game makes no network requests of its own. Google's advertising and billing libraries add the permissions they need to work, such as reading the advertising identifier. The game does not ask for your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address collected
  • No analytics, crash reporting service or attribution SDK
  • No servers of our own — the game never talks to us
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone
  • No tracking across other apps or websites by us

Children

Magic Crystal Shatter is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data has reached the advertising SDK, write to us and we will help you take it up with Google.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase. To remove everything the game stores, uninstall it. To reset the advertising identifier, use the device settings listed under Advertising. To change your consent choice in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, clear the app's data (Android) or reinstall the game, and the consent form will appear again — note that this also erases your progress, because it lives only on your device.

Changes
