Magic Crystal Shatter — Support

Onsaga Softworks · a puzzle game about shattering crystals

Fastest way to get help: onsagasoftworks@gmail.com. Tell us your device and what happened.

How the game works

Tap a group of three or more touching crystals of the same colour to shatter them. Bigger groups hit harder, and tapping again quickly builds a combo. Each level has a goal bar to fill before you run out of moves; tapping crystals that do not form a group more than once in a row costs you some of that progress. As you go, special stones start to appear — each one is explained the first time you meet it — and bombs, shuffles and extra moves can help when you are stuck.

I got a new phone. Where is my progress?

On the device you played on. Magic Crystal Shatter has no accounts, no servers and no cloud save, so we hold no copy of your progress. If your phone's own backup is switched on — Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone — and you set up the new phone from that backup, the game's save may come back with it. Otherwise a new phone starts fresh. What you bought is part of that save too — see below.

I paid for something and it is gone

Everything in the shop is consumable. Gem packs and the one-time Starter Bundle go straight into your balance on your device as soon as the store confirms the payment, so they belong to your save rather than to your App Store or Google Play account. That is why there is no "restore purchases" button: there is nothing held on your account to bring back. If the save is lost — a new phone set up without a device backup, or a reinstall — what you bought goes with it.

If you were charged but the gems or items never appeared, email us with the store receipt and we will sort it out.

Can I turn the ads off?

There is nothing to turn off: the game never shows an advert you did not ask for. The only adverts are videos you can choose to watch — to double your coins at the end of a level, to get extra moves when you run out, or, now and then when you are short, for free gems. If you would rather not watch, skip the offer: take your normal coins, continue with coins instead, or try the level again. That is also why there is no "remove ads" purchase.

The game froze / crashed

If you leave in the middle of a level — a call, switching apps, closing the game — your board is saved. Open the game again and tap RESUME on the main menu to carry on where you stopped. The pause button at the top of the board also lets you continue, restart or go back to the menu.

If it actually crashed, please email us your device model, OS version and what you were doing. There is no crash reporting service in the game, so we only learn about a bug if someone tells us.

How do I delete everything the game stores?

Uninstall it. Everything lives on the device, so removing the app removes all of it; there is no copy on a server to ask us to delete. If your phone backs up app data to Google or iCloud, that copy is managed from your device's backup settings.

Privacy

No accounts, no servers of our own and no analytics; the only data collected is what Google AdMob needs to show adverts. The full policy is at privacy.html.
