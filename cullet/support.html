Fastest way to get help: onsagasoftworks@gmail.com. Tell us your device and what happened; we read everything.

How the game works

You are a spark orbiting a bright core, ringed by shells of glass. Hold to charge, let go to fire yourself outward. How long you held is how deep you cut: charge for one pane and you break one, charge for four and you go through four. Break nothing and the run is over.

I got a new phone. Where is my progress?

It stays on the old one. Cullet has no accounts and no servers — your score, shards, sparks and achievements are written to the device you played on and nowhere else. That is deliberate: no sign-up, nothing to leak. It does mean a new phone starts fresh.

Purchases are the exception. Those belong to your App Store or Google Play account, not to the device, so they can be brought back — see below.

I paid for something and it is gone

Open Settings (the gear at the top right of the menu) and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Anything permanent — the starter pack, remove ads — comes back.

Shard packs are consumable: they were spent into your balance when you bought them, so there is nothing to restore. If a purchase was charged but never arrived, email us with the store receipt and we will sort it out.

How do I remove the ads?

Shop → Shards tab → Remove ads. One purchase, permanent, and it stops the advert between runs for good. The optional advert you can choose to watch to continue a run stays available, because it is a favour rather than an interruption.

What can I buy, and what does it change?

  • Sparks are colour — the ball you play and the trail behind it. They change nothing about how the game plays.
  • Boosters are single use and change one run: a shield, a longer combo window, a doubled score.
  • Both are bought with shards, and shards come from the daily reward or from the shop. Playing pays you a score, not a currency.

The daily reward

Open the game on consecutive days and it grows, from 30 shards up to 120. Miss a day and the streak starts again. It is for opening the app, not for playing it, so a short visit counts.

The game froze / crashed

If the game was interrupted mid-run — a call, a screenshot, switching apps — it pauses and shows TAP TO CONTINUE. Tap anywhere to carry on; nothing is lost.

If it actually crashed, reopening it shows what went wrong. Sending us that message, with your device model and OS version, is the single most useful thing you can do — there is no crash reporting service watching, so we only learn about a bug if someone tells us.

How do I delete everything the game stores?

Uninstall it. Everything lives on the device, so removing the app removes all of it. There is no copy on a server to ask us to delete.
