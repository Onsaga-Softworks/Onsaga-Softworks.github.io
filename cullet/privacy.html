Privacy Policy — Cullet

Last updated 3 September 2026 · Onsaga Softworks · com.onsagasoftworks.cullet

The short version. Cullet has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your progress is written to your own device and nowhere else. The only data that leaves your phone is what the advertising SDK collects to show adverts, and what Apple or Google handles when you make a purchase. We never see your name, your email or your payment details.

What stays on your device

Everything the game remembers about you is stored locally, on the phone or tablet you play on. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared:

Stored asWhat it holds
cullet.profileShards, which sparks you own, which one is equipped, boosters held, daily-reward streak, run count
cullet.bestYour highest score
cullet.statsLifetime totals — games played, panes broken, best chain, best combo — which is what unlocks achievements
cullet.marksWhether you have seen the tutorial
cullet.lastCrashThe last error message, if the game ever crashed, so it can show you what happened
cullet.resetIdA number used to reset a save between development builds

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on request and nothing to restore if you reinstall.

Advertising

Cullet shows adverts between runs, and an optional advert you may choose to watch to carry on after a death. These are served by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob collects device-level data — typically an advertising identifier, IP address, coarse device and OS information, and how you interacted with the advert. That collection is Google's, under Google's privacy policy, not ours: we receive no personal data from it.

  • You can reset or clear your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for consent through Google's own consent form before requesting any advert. If you refuse, no advert is requested at all. If you allow basic adverts but refuse personalisation, only non-personalised adverts are requested.
  • Buying Remove ads stops adverts, and with them the SDK's collection.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. Your payment details go to them and never pass through the game. The game receives only a confirmation that a purchase completed, and unlocks what you bought on your device. We do not know who bought what.

Permissions

Cullet asks for one Android permission, MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS, which lets the game's sound behave properly alongside other audio. It explicitly declines the microphone, external storage and draw-over-other-apps permissions. It has no access to your camera, contacts, files, location or messages.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address collected
  • No analytics, crash reporting service, or attribution SDK
  • No servers of our own — the game never talks to us
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone
  • No tracking across other apps or websites by us

Children

Cullet is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the advertising SDK, contact us and we will help you raise it with Google.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase. To remove everything the game stores, uninstall it. To deal with the advertising identifier, use the device settings above, or withdraw consent by clearing the app's data (Android) or reinstalling, which makes the consent form appear again.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version replaces this page. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
